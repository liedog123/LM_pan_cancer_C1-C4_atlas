The D1 candidate-set source is author-confirmed Wang et al. (2024), doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113796; individual Function annotations were imported from the source workbook and their original attribution remains unverified. D3/D4 are study-curated mappings/tiers over local ImmPort terms (twelve paper-matched families plus five acid–TME extensions). A subsequent methodological review points to Xia et al., Nature Immunology 25, 659–670 (2024), doi:10.1038/s41590-024-01789-x; this is not the original table-creation log or row-by-row provenance. Later Reactome/ImmuCellAI/C7 catalogues are distinct.

The C marker selection is byte-identical to the adopted R01 C selection and retains all 36 unique genes and eight original fields. The D selection retains its native comparison, pathway, pathway_display, pathway_order columns and 15 unique IDs. Its ID set, first-occurrence order and per-ID display labels match the historical 60-row D statistical table, so the same non-statistical selection supplies 12/15 without changing either R consumer. Duplicate display names do not imply duplicate IDs; do not deduplicate by label. The compressed statistical table is not bundled. GMTs and patient-level prepared inputs remain external prerequisites.

Changing RTK alone would not directly change those five relationships' rho or raw P values, but could change their FDR, significance markers and significant-cancer counts. The CNV and six immune rows use separate BH families. A 2026-10-06 R 4.5.1 local sensitivity comparison added HER3_pY1289 with positive sign to the historical six-member RTK score. Fig. 5C's first five rows retained all 100 evaluable rho values, significance markers and significant-cancer counts (11/9/8/5/5), although FDR values changed. RPPA orthogonal-candidate membership was unchanged.
